CLASSROOM SEQUENCE
Game -> independent assessment A-D -> discussion and experimental contrast.
Students make initial responses individually. Evidence remains available, but supplied reasons appear only AFTER the original explanation is saved/skipped.
For paper practice, release Stage 1 first and collect/check it before Stage 2; Stage 2 contains supplied reasons. Release Stage 3 after the editorial decision.
Do not show the assessment key or debrief example before independent responses are saved.
The working game target is 20-25 minutes; verify it. For initial scheduling, allow up to 30 minutes for the game, 15 for the expanded assessment, and 5 for discussion. These are provisional allowances, not measured times.

RECORDS
Nothing uploads or submits automatically. Download CSV/JSON or print if requested.
Game CSV: one row per attempt. Original reasoning and supplied-choice responses are distinct. First/final evidence-note drafts remain separate. Student writing is not automatically graded or approved.
Reflection: typed responses = saved; paper/oral completion self-report = external_reported; skipped = skipped.
Paper/oral content and facilitator verification are NOT stored in the game. Keep and verify the separate paper/facilitator record. The game records the reported mode, not a verified external response.
Assessment CSV: FOUR rows for ONE student's A-D record. Optional experience ratings repeat on each row; analyze once per student. Skipped cases export as missing. record_submitted differs from assessment_complete (all four cases answered).
No automatic scores are supplied on the student assessment. The guide contains proposed rubrics.
If you need to link files, use your approved facilitator procedure; game and assessment do not automatically match records.

EVALUATION LIMITS
This is guided practice plus a small independent assessment. Completion, enjoyment, changing one's mind, and reflection length are not evidence of learning.
The package does not establish intervention effectiveness, broad misinformation resistance, calibration, lasting transfer, or a PCT mechanism.
It does not include the matched plain lesson, expanded alternate assessment forms, baseline/follow-up scheduling, consent collection, or automatic research uploads.
Read research-changes.html for functional checks. Test your classroom devices and assistive technology before use.
