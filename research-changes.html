How the research shaped this update

This update applies the attached Research lessons for the SIB Evidence Lab, dated 2 November 2026. It combines the current GitHub site’s dark lab layout with revised decision-making tasks. The current GitHub version supplied a corrected bulletin automatically; this version requires students to select an accurate headline and relevant evidence before completing their newsletter item.

Research access and claims

The supplied review covers 53 research publications and 11 resources. It reports 25 research sources accessed through relevant full-text sections, 6 through substantial excerpts, and 22 through abstracts only; the 11 resources include 8 substantive guides and 3 overviews. These are 64 sources, not 64 fully read papers or independent experiments.

This update applies that supplied synthesis. It does not present a new systematic review or imply that all source texts were independently reread for this build. The design still requires testing with students. Original SIB pilot results do not validate this new game.
