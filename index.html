STUDENT LEARNING COMES FIRST

A clear purpose for every activity.

Alan and Alex are interested in these projects as opportunities for students to learn useful skills. Each completed activity should give students a clear goal, a chance to practice, useful feedback, and a final task that applies the idea.

SIB is ready to try in this review edition. Its final check asks students to distinguish confidence from correctness, distinguish authenticity from truth, and select a verification step before sharing.

Prepared for review by Dr. Alan Franklin and Dr. Alex Varakin at EKU, with Prof. Belle Woodward at SIU.
