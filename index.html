About this activity

This educational adaptation uses two of the Session 2 SIB example clips, separate from experimental Sets A and B. It adds new judgments and confidence ratings for a self-guided activity. The original Session 2 procedure uses the clips for feedback and structured reflection.

Answer labels follow the SIB Session 2 internal example record and final selection criteria. They have not been independently reauthenticated for this activity. “Real” refers to the clip’s authenticity category, not the truth of every statement in it.

Original video files are preserved without recompression. This page does not transmit or save responses. This educational activity explores confidence and judgments about digital media.
