Why psychology and human factors matter

Feeling certain, recognizing a face or logo, expecting a particular answer, and seeing others share can influence a digital decision. Interface design also affects which evidence is easy to find. Ask what influenced your first impression and what evidence could change it. A mistake alone does not diagnose a psychological bias.

The clips are visual observation exercises with unresolved provenance. The evidence missions use clearly marked fictional practice records. This activity does not measure your deepfake detection ability.

TaskConfidenceOutcome
Human factors: look back at your decisions
  • Metacognition: did your certainty match the strength of your evidence?
  • Familiarity: did a face, logo, or polished presentation influence your impression?
  • Expectations: what evidence would challenge your first explanation?
  • Social influence: how did popularity or urgency invite you to act?
  • Interface design: what made the evidence easy or difficult to compare?
  • Agency: what useful choice remained when you could not be certain?

These are questions for reflection, not diagnoses of your thinking.
